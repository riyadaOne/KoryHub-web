# "Verified in Prod" certification

The "Verified in Prod" badge means that a person from the moderation team has reviewed the architecture and documentation of a specific version. It is always granted by a person, never automatically.

## What the badge guarantees

A declared and consistent architecture, and documentation that explains the problem and the pitfalls. The badge covers **one version**: a new version is not certified until it has been reviewed.

## The 5 preliminary checks

Before the request, KoryHub checks that:

1. the architecture is declared (`kory graph`);
2. the README contains "Problem solved" and "Pitfalls avoided";
3. the license is on the official list;
4. the version is published, not deprecated, removed or at risk;
5. no case is already open for this module.

The module must also be public and your email address verified.

## Request

From the version page: "Request the certification". Your cases are in your dashboard, under "My certifications".

## Complement, decision, report

The team may ask you for more information: answer with a message, or offer a new version (the checks run again). You are told when the decision is made. A granted certification produces a PDF report in your language and in English, downloadable from the version page.

## Revocation

The badge can be revoked, with a reason, if a problem is found. The report can still be downloaded, but the version page is the reference.

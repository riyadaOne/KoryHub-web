# Governance and transparency

How KoryHub is moderated, and what is never automatic.

## Who moderates

The moderation team of RIYADA ONE TECHNOLOGIES SARL. Every decision has a reason and stays in a log that nobody, not even the team, can change or erase.

## Report

Any account with a verified email address can report a module, a version, an account or a review, for malware, illegal content, copyright or harassment. The reported person never knows who reported them. You follow your reports in your settings and you are told the decision.

## What moderation can do

- Close a report with no action.
- Add a security warning to a version: it is shown on the module page and in `kory audit`.
- Remove a version for a legal reason.
- Hide a review.
- Suspend or delete an account.

People who follow or use a version are told about a warning or a removal.

## Certification

The "Verified in Prod" badge is granted by a person from the team after reviewing the architecture and documentation of a version. It can be revoked with a reason. The detailed rules are in the [documentation](https://docs.koryhub.dev/en/certification).

## Legal removal

A version removed for a legal reason can no longer be downloaded and its files are deleted. Its page remains, with the date of removal and the archive checksum. A removed version never comes back.

## Account suspension

A suspended account can no longer log in or use its tokens. Its public modules can still be downloaded, so the projects that use them keep working. The reason is sent by email.

## What is never automatic

- No module is hidden because it was reported, even several times: a person always reviews.
- Certification and featuring on the home page are human decisions.
- The CLI never installs or updates itself.

## Contact

For a legal removal request or a question about moderation, use the report option on the page concerned.

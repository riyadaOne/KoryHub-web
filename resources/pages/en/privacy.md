# Privacy

Updated on 14 November 2026.

## Controller

RIYADA ONE TECHNOLOGIES SARL, N'Djamena (Chad), publishes KoryHub and decides how the data described here is used.

## Data collected

- **Account**: public handle, name (optional), email address, country (optional), photo (optional), language, encrypted password.
- **Security**: logins, CLI tokens (never their clear value), passkeys, two-factor authentication.
- **Activity**: published modules, reviews, stars, follows, reports, downloads made while logged in.
- **Technical**: IP address and CLI version, for security and download limits.

## Why

To run the service, protect accounts, moderate, and notify the people concerned by a version (update, deprecation, security warning).

## Retention periods

Account data is kept as long as the account exists. The audit log is kept with no time limit, without personal data: a personal field appears as "changed", without its value.

## What the CLI never collects

The CLI never sends folder names, paths on your computer, Git repository addresses or machine names. It only identifies itself by its version, the operating system and the processor.

## Your rights

You see your data in your settings (profile, security log, my activity, my downloads). You can correct it, and delete your account: your personal data is then erased. Your public modules remain under your handle, so the projects that use them keep working.

## Processors

Hosting of the website and database, storage of archives and sending of emails are entrusted to providers, bound by contract and chosen for their security.
